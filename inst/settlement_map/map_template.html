Settlement types: Ringing Census 2026

    About this map

    Each dot is a tower in the census sampling frame, coloured by the draft settlement type it is given. Larger dots have more bells. Click a dot for the measures behind its type. The types are provisional: see the analysis chapter Choosing a classification.

    Densities are people per km² of land, with nearer people counting more: around the church, the weight halves every 500 m; for the district, used only for the inner-city line, every 1 km. An urban centre is a continuous built-up area of 50,000 or more, as traced by the international Degree of Urbanisation.
